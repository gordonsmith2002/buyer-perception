## Design notes

- Dark: charcoal `#252626` field, inverted lockup / terracotta mark
- White: white `#FFFFFF` field, primary lockup / terracotta mark
- Terracotta `#B94E30` accents
- Profile banners: content starts ~300px from the left for the circular profile photo
- Company banners: centered lockup; keep critical art away from the far edges
